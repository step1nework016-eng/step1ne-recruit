# Creator Type Classification Guide

## personal_koc

Typical signals:
- clearly a real individual;
- content centers on personal experience/lifestyle/reviews;
- smaller or mid-sized audience;
- first-person bio/content;
- may have collaboration contact information.

## creator

Typical signals:
- individual professional content creator;
- consistent niche/topic output;
- not necessarily positioned as a large influencer.

## influencer

Typical signals:
- individual with clear public influence/large audience;
- frequent partnerships/sponsorships;
- creator identity is commercially mature.

## brand_account

Typical signals:
- account represents a company/product brand;
- bio uses corporate/brand language;
- product/store information dominates;
- no clear individual owner/creator persona.

## store_account

Typical signals:
- direct sales/storefront orientation;
- product catalog, ordering, shipping, store address;
- account exists primarily to sell inventory.

Common Taiwanese-market bio phrases that indicate store_account even when the account also posts
lifestyle-style content and follows a normal number of accounts (don't require following=0 as a
gate — a store can follow suppliers/other shops just like anyone else): 賣場經營, 板娘 (shop
owner/admin), 開立電子發票 (issues formal invoices — implies registered commercial retail), 代購
(purchasing agent/reseller), 選物 (curated-goods shop), 直播零售 (livestream retail), 直購, 百貨,
客服 (customer service). A bio combining several of these with a product catalog is store_account
even if the surface content looks like personal "開箱"/reviews — real KOC accounts don't usually
also mention issuing invoices or having customer service.

## media_account

Typical signals:
- publication, community media, news, curation, magazine, fan/media page;
- content is published as an organization rather than one creator.

## unknown

Use when available public information is insufficient for a reliable classification.

## Decision rule

Classification should reflect what the account *is*, not merely what content appears once.

When uncertain between a valid creator type and an excluded commercial account, prefer `unknown` and avoid reporting until enough evidence exists.
